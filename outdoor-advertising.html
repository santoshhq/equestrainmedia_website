Outdoor marketing agency

Planned byroute, not bysite list.

Hyderabad | Telangana | Andhra Pradesh Sites booked, printed and installed by our own team.

Outdoor advertising in India is still mostly bought as a list of sites. We start with the audience's route instead — where they live, the corridor they drive and the mall they end up in — and place billboard, street and mobile formats along it so the brand is seen more than once on the same day.

Our outdoor marketing inventory covers front-lit and backlit hoardings, flex and round-flex sites on dividers, pole kiosks along arterial roads, digital wall posters, no-parking boards and the mall façade positions at LuLu Mall, Hyderabad. For moving reach we add LED vans, Tata Ace vehicles and auto branding from our mobile advertising fleet.

We also take on the parts that usually slow an outdoor campaign down: municipal permissions, printing, night installation windows, maintenance and dated photographs of every site as proof of display.

What does an outdoor advertising agency in India actually do?

An outdoor advertising agency plans which sites a brand should appear on, negotiates and books them, produces and installs the creative, and maintains it for the campaign period. Equestrian Media does all of this from Hyderabad, with permissions, printing, night installation and photographic proof of display handled in-house.

What is the difference between billboard advertising and hoardings?

In India the words are used interchangeably. A hoarding or billboard is a large fixed panel — front-lit or backlit — on a building, a gantry or a roadside structure. We operate flex and round-flex hoardings on roads and dividers, plus the five façade hoarding positions on LuLu Mall, Hyderabad.

Which cities does Equestrian Media cover for outdoor marketing?

Hyderabad is our home market and where our owned and managed inventory sits. We plan and execute outdoor marketing campaigns across Telangana and Andhra Pradesh, and can extend a mobile-media route into surrounding districts.

How is an outdoor advertising campaign measured?

Outdoor advertising is measured by exposure rather than clicks. We plan against traffic corridors and footfall data — for example the 25,000+ daily visitors at LuLu Mall — and report with dated installation and monitoring photographs for every site, so you can see exactly what ran and when.
