Hyderabad | Telangana | Andhra Pradesh Activations staffed and supervised by our own team.

Brand activation is where a campaign stops being a message and becomes an experience. As an experiential marketing agency, Equestrian Media plans the idea, secures the space, produces the set-up and staffs the activation — so a launch, a test-drive display or a sampling drive runs as one piece of work rather than five separate vendors.

Most of our brand activation work happens inside high-footfall environments: the central atrium and kiosk positions at LuLu Mall, Hyderabad, where we hold official advertising rights, alongside retail promotions, corporate events and street-level BTL activation across Telangana and Andhra Pradesh.

Experiential advertising agencies are usually judged on the idea. We are also judged on the day: the display is up on time, the emcee has the brief, the samples are stocked, and afterwards you get photographs from the floor, not a summary.

What is the difference between brand activation and experiential marketing?

They overlap. Experiential marketing is the broader idea of letting people experience a brand rather than just see it. A brand activation is the specific event that does it — a product display, a sampling drive, a stage engagement or a launch set-up in a mall atrium. Equestrian Media plans the experience and runs the activation as one piece of work.

Where does Equestrian Media run brand activations?

Most of our activations run in high-footfall retail environments in Hyderabad — including the central atrium and kiosk positions at LuLu Mall, Hyderabad, where we hold official advertising rights — as well as retail promotions, corporate events and on-ground BTL activation across Telangana and Andhra Pradesh.

Can a brand activation be combined with other media?

Yes, and it usually should be. An atrium activation can be supported by the main atrium LED screen, façade hoardings, lift-lobby backlit boards and cinema advertising in the same building, so the audience sees the brand on the way in, at the activation and on the way out. We plan these together in one media plan.

How much lead time does a mall activation need?

Space availability is confirmed at the time of booking, and every activation needs time for space confirmation, mall approvals, artwork sign-off, production and manpower briefing. Share your preferred dates as early as you can and we will confirm what is achievable.
