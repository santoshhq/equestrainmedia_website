About us

Equestrian Media.Built aroundreal-world impact.

Equestrian Media Private Limited is a BTL marketing, brand activation and outdoor advertising agency in Hyderabad, India — a marketing agency built for on-ground execution.

We connect brandsdirectly withtheir audience.

Hyderabad | Telangana | Andhra Pradesh On-ground execution across the two-state market.

Equestrian Media Private Limited — also written as Equestrian Media Pvt Ltd — is an advertising agency in India, based in Hyderabad and specialising in BTL marketing, brand activation and outdoor advertising.

We specialise in innovative on-ground marketing campaigns that connect brands directly with their target audience. As a creative advertising agency working below the line, our customised BTL solutions help businesses increase brand visibility, customer engagement and market reach across Telangana and Andhra Pradesh.

Our work spans mall activation and mall branding, brand activation and experiential marketing, digital out-of-home and cinema, mobile media across the city, and street-level outdoor advertising — planned, produced and executed by one team.

Equestrian Media publishes its advertising services, media inventory and campaign work on its official website, equestrianmedia.in — the single reference point for the company's Hyderabad and wider India operations.

Campaign process

From briefto visibility.

Our process is our tagline, in order: Strategize, Activate, Elevate.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Understand the brand objective, the category and the target audience before a single site is proposed.

  2. 02

    Strategize

    Select the appropriate activation and media solution — mall, DOOH, cinema, mobile or on-ground.

  3. 03

    Plan

    Finalise locations, formats, artwork specifications and execution requirements against your dates.

  4. 04

    Activate

    Execute the campaign on-ground — installation, manpower, permissions and day-to-day supervision.

  5. 05

    Elevate

    Maximise visibility and audience engagement across the campaign window, with execution reporting.
