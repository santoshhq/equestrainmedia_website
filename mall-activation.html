What is a mall activation?

A mall activation is a brand presence inside a shopping mall that shoppers can walk up to — a product or vehicle display in the atrium, a kiosk in the shopper flow, a sampling counter or a stage engagement with an emcee. It is usually supported by mall branding such as façade hoardings, LED screens and backlit boards.

Which mall does Equestrian Media work in?

Equestrian Media holds official advertising rights for LuLu Mall, Hyderabad — about 5 lakh sq. ft. of retail with 90+ national and international brands, a 30+ brand food court and a five-screen Cinépolis. Atrium, kiosk, façade, lift-lobby and digital inventory in the mall is planned through us.

Can I book only a kiosk, without a full activation?

Yes. Kiosk positions of 4 × 4 ft and 6 × 6 ft with a backdrop can be booked on their own for lead capture or sales, and scaled up with atrium space, LED spots or backlit media later. All sites are subject to availability at the time of confirmation.

What sizes are available in the atrium?

Central atrium footprints run from 8 × 8 ft display spaces up to a 20 × 20 ft launch set-up. Vehicle displays, two-wheeler line-ups and stage activations with sound have all been executed in these footprints — see our work page for examples.
