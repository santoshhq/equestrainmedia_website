About Equestrian Media

Built aroundreal-world impact.

Hyderabad | Telangana | Andhra Pradesh On-ground execution across the two-state market.

Equestrian Media Private Limited is a BTL marketing, brand activation and outdoor advertising agency based in Hyderabad, India.

Most agencies book media. We also hold it: official advertising rights at LuLu Mall, Hyderabad, the LED vans, autos and promotional fleets we operate, and a team that plans, installs and supervises the campaign. That is what lets a brand run an activation, a façade hoarding and a DOOH loop as one campaign rather than three vendors — across Hyderabad, Telangana and Andhra Pradesh.

Campaign process

From briefto visibility.

Our process is our tagline, in order: Strategize, Activate, Elevate.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Understand the brand objective, the category and the target audience before a single site is proposed.

  2. 02

    Strategize

    Select the appropriate activation and media solution — mall, DOOH, cinema, mobile or on-ground.

  3. 03

    Plan

    Finalise locations, formats, artwork specifications and execution requirements against your dates.

  4. 04

    Activate

    Execute the campaign on-ground — installation, manpower, permissions and day-to-day supervision.

  5. 05

    Elevate

    Maximise visibility and audience engagement across the campaign window, with execution reporting.
